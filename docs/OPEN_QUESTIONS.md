# Open questions

Only items that change the next milestone are listed. Row spacing and row count can wait until row detection; they must be metadata, not guessed constants.

## Blocks Phase 1

1. **Photographs.** Where will the first real microplot images be placed for the feasibility pass? The pipeline has nothing agronomic to measure until they exist.
2. **Borders.** What marks the plot edge in those photos (board, string, tape, bare soil, paint, or only a change in plants)? This decides whether automatic boundaries are worth trying or corners should be manual from the start.
3. **Dimensions.** For each photographed plot, what are the known length and width in metres? Without them the scale experiment cannot succeed, and it should be reported as unavailable rather than skipped silently.
4. **Growth stage.** What stage will the first photos show? Emergence, early tillering, and a closed canopy need different expectations for rows and for whether a mask is even interpretable. This does not block loading the images; it blocks interpreting them.

## Explicitly deferred

- Exact row count and row spacing, until Phase 2.
- Drone model and DJI software, until a pilot needs aerial images.
- AWS credit balance, expiration, and eligible services, until Phase 5.
- Whether one photo resolves individual plants, until a counting experiment is justified.
